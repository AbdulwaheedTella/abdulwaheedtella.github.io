Reliable GeoAI with the area of applicability

Summary

Machine-learning flood maps are usually applied across a whole city, including places that can look very different from the training data. This work, developed during a research placement at Curtin University, applies the area of applicability (AoA) to ensemble flood susceptibility models in Kuala Lumpur, so the map marks where predictions can be trusted and where they are extrapolations. Companion studies test whether more complex ensemble and hybrid models really improve flood mapping, and review how validation is done in the literature. The papers are under review; results will be added here once they are published.
