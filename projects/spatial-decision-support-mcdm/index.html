Spatial decision support with multi-criteria analysis

Applies GIS and multi-criteria decision analysis to flood susceptibility, highway landslide risk and landfill siting, turning spatial data into ranked, defensible options.

Problem
Siting and risk decisions involve several competing criteria. Decision-makers need a transparent way to combine spatial layers into a ranking they can defend.
My role
Lead or contributing author
Period
2017 to 2024
Key contribution
Ensemble fuzzy MCDM for flood susceptibility, a comparative assessment of data mining, MCDM and fuzzy computing, and GIS-based MCDM for highway landslide proneness and landfill suitability.
Outcome
Four Q1 journal articles, from the 2020 Natural Hazards flood susceptibility paper to the 2024 landfill suitability study in Lagos.
Methods and tools
  • ArcGIS Pro
  • QGIS
  • Multi-criteria decision making (MCDM)
  • Fuzzy methods
  • Remote sensing

How the work flows

  1. 1 Criteria Spatial layers and factors
  2. 2 Weighting MCDM, fuzzy, AHP
  3. 3 Overlay GIS suitability or hazard surface
  4. 4 Decision Ranked areas and corridors

Context

Where the question is “which site, which corridor, which area first”, a transparent multi-criteria analysis can be more useful than a black-box model. This is where my work started: my undergraduate dissertation was flood susceptibility mapping with GIS and MCDM.

Applications

  • Flood susceptibility. An ensemble fuzzy MCDM approach for Ibadan, Nigeria (Natural Hazards, 2020), and a comparison of data mining, multi-criteria decision making and fuzzy computing for spatial flood susceptibility (Geocarto International, 2022).
  • Infrastructure corridor risk. A highway proneness appraisal for landslides along the Taiping to Ipoh segment in Malaysia using MCDM and GIS (Sustainability, 2022).
  • Facility siting. Data-driven landfill suitability mapping for Lagos State with GIS-based MCDM (International Journal of Environmental Science and Technology, 2024).
