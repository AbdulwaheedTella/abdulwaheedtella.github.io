Countries in Southeast Asia are adopting AI at very different speeds. Funded by an APNIC Foundation research fellowship (USD 10,000), this multi-country, mixed-methods study maps AI and digital-technology capability gaps across Asia-Pacific institutions, identifies the institutional barriers to adoption, and sets out evidence-based capacity-building strategies towards a regional policy roadmap. The accompanying systematic review is under review; findings will be added here once published.
