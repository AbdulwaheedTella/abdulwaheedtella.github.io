## Context

This project, _Mapping AI Capability Disparities Across ASEAN: Towards a Regional Policy Roadmap_, moves away from building models to asking who is able to use them. It was supported by an APNIC Foundation research fellowship (2025).

## Approach

- **Interdisciplinary framing.** The study looked at AI and digital-technology capability gaps across Asia-Pacific institutions, bridging geospatial analytics with AI governance and digital infrastructure policy.
- **Institutional barriers.** It investigated the barriers that slow AI adoption and proposed evidence-based capacity-building strategies.
- **Team and method.** I led the work as Principal Investigator and supervised two research assistants in mixed-methods data collection, analysis and reporting.
- **Policy translation.** The findings contribute to recommendations for closing the AI readiness gap in Southeast Asia. I presented on these themes as an expert panellist at the International Higher Education Policy Dialogue in March 2026.

## Status

The fellowship ran from November 2025 to April 2026. The associated systematic review is under review, so results are not reported on this page yet.
